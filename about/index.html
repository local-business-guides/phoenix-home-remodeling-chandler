Design first, build second

Phoenix Home Remodeling is a design-build remodeler serving Chandler and the Phoenix metro. We specialize in kitchen, bathroom, shower, and whole home interior remodels. Our in-house team manages design, planning, and construction from start to finish.

Every project starts with feasibility, 3D design, and finalized material selections. You'll approve the design and receive your final construction price before demolition begins. This planning approach is intended to reduce surprises, scope gaps, and change orders by resolving project details before construction.

We've completed more than 1,000 kitchen and bathroom remodels. Phoenix Home Remodeling is a NARI and NKBA member and has been licensed since 2017, ROC#313636.

Prepare for your conversation

Have your property location, the rooms you'd like to remodel, and your main questions ready when you call. Photos or inspiration examples can help explain your preferences. If you're planning to come to our address, call first to arrange the visit and confirm what to bring.

Planning a Chandler kitchen project

Kitchen remodeling starts with feasibility rather than demolition. Our process includes 3D design and finalized material selections, followed by your approval of the design and a final construction price. Preparing for an initial conversation

You don't need a completed design to explain your goals. A short description of the space, photos of its current condition, and notes about your preferred changes can help organize the discussion. Mention any plans to remodel adjoining rooms, too. That information helps establish whether you're considering a kitchen project or a broader interior renovation.
